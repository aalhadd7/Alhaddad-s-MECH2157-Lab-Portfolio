Ameer Alhaddad About Me:

Since I was a kid I have always been drawn to doing things with my hands. I loved building legos and trying to organize and fix small things around the house. As I got older I became fond of construction and design. Thinking of new things to build and create to be able to introduce that to the world and help people out has always a source of joy for me. 

I have a solid works certificate. I did two projects; the first one was following a manual on how to design a flashlight, and the second one was free hand designing the different parts of a lego set and putting them all together on the software. I hope in this class and future classes I will be able to actually print things I design. But for this class I would like to strengthen my CAD and design skills. 

I believe as engineers we need to be able to be ok with failing and learning. For me failure is something you need to achieve. By pushing your own boundaries and expanding what you already know so that you come out stronger and smarter. Engineers are innovative and persistent on wanting change.
